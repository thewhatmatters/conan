## AC

1. Visible Sagan surface polls/refreshes the ledger automatically.
2. Needs-you dock badge updates without manual refresh.
3. No jarring loading flash during background refresh.
4. Last-updated timestamp shown and refreshed.
5. Network errors are surfaced gracefully (do not crash the surface).
6. Polling stops when the surface is hidden or the project changes.

## Non-goals

- Bidirectional push/WebSocket infrastructure (future optimization).
- Writing back to the ledger from the UI (C5).

## Related

- WHA-152 (C2 design pass may specify refresh affordance / timestamp treatment).
- WHA-154 (C3 inspector design pass).

## Frontend

- `useSaganCapability` performs a project-scoped discovery read, then polls every
  7.5 seconds while Sagan is the active surface.
- Background refreshes retain the last successful ledger projection, update the
  needs-you count and timestamp atomically, and show retry errors without
  replacing the overview with a loading state.
- Hiding Sagan, changing projects, or unmounting clears the timer and aborts the
  active request.
